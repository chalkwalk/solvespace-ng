**Goal:** M5's "done when" (AGENTS.md section 8): a printed-hinge model drags live and
reports correct clearance.

**Architecture** (AGENTS.md section 7: "motion joints move Parts away from the design pose
without rebuilding their geometry; dragging a mechanism is a pure solve plus a mesh
transform"):
- **Joints are records** (`SK.joint`, `Joint`, saved as `AddJoint`), each with:
  - a type: revolute or slider;
  - a parent part and a child part;
  - an axis entity on each;
  - a value (degrees or mm; 0 is the design pose);
  - optional limits (low and high);
  - a name.

  Undo snapshots them as it does parts.
- **Making a joint.** Select two entities on two different parts, then New Group ▸
  Revolute Joint (`joint-revolute`) or Slider Joint (`joint-slider`).
  - An axis comes from a line segment (its points), a circle or arc (centre and normal),
    or a face (point and normal).
  - A revolute's two axes must be the same line in the design pose, and a slider's must be
    parallel; otherwise the command refuses with a message.
  - The parent is the part earlier in part order, the child the other.
  - A part has at most one joint to a parent, so the joints form a tree.
- **The pose.** Each part's pose is a rigid transform (rotation q, translation t),
  computed from the joint tree: `T(child) = T(parent) ∘ J(value)`.
  - J is the rotation about the parent's axis, or the translation along it.
  - `SolveSpaceUI::PartPose(hPart)`.
  - Kinematic loops (a four-bar) would need the solver: later.
- **Display and picking.** A group whose part is posed draws and picks a transformed copy
  of its display mesh and outlines (`Group::ShownMesh()`, `ShownOutlines()`), remade when
  the pose changes.
  - Sketch entities, exports, mass properties and the parts list stay in the design pose.
  - Analyze ▸ Design Pose (`design-pose`) sets every joint back to 0.
- **Moving a part:**
  - **Properties of a joint:** its value (typed), limits, a scrub slider, and "sweep
    clearance".
  - **The Outliner:** lists the joints under a "Joints" header; clicking a joint shows it in
    Properties.
  - **`G` on a face of a jointed part:** drags the joint. A revolute's angle follows the
    mouse around its axis; a slider's distance follows it along the axis.
  - **Grab details:** typing a value sets the change; Esc restores; limits clamp.
- **Clearance sweep** (`SolveSpaceUI::SweepClearance(hJoint)`):
  - The moving side is the child and the parts under it; every other part with a solid is
    still.
  - The joint is sampled across its range: its limits, else a full turn; a slider needs
    limits.
  - Each sample takes the B-reps' minimum distance (`KernelMinDistance`, OCCT
    `BRepExtrema_DistShapeShape` on located shapes). The smallest sample is refined by
    golden section.
  - It reports "minimum gap 0.20 mm at 47°", and the ranges where the parts touch or
    overlap ("collide from 80° to 100°").

## Global constraints
As before. Old files open and save byte-identical (no joint, no record). OCCT-only code is
guarded, and the sweep says it needs Open CASCADE otherwise.

## Review focus
1. A joint whose axis entity is deleted: the joint is dropped with its entity (as constraints
   are), not left dangling.
2. A child part with no solid: posing it changes nothing; the sweep says there is nothing
   to sweep.
3. Undo after a scrub or a grab: the value returns.
4. Saving in a motion pose: the value is saved; reopening shows the same pose.
5. A part that is the child of a joint and the parent of another: both move when the first
   joint turns.
