## Tasks

### Task 1: joints with more than one motion

- `Joint` keeps up to three motions (`value[3]`, `limited[3]`, `lo[3]`, `hi[3]`; DOF 0 keeps
  the M5a keys `Joint.value`, `Joint.limited`, `Joint.lo`, `Joint.hi`; DOFs 1 and 2 save as
  `Joint.value1` and so on).
- New types:
  - **Cylindrical** (`joint-cylindrical`): turn and slide about one shared line.
  - **Planar** (`joint-planar`): slide in a shared plane (u, v) and turn about its normal.
    The axes are faces or lines read as normals; they must be parallel.
  - **Ball** (`joint-ball`): turns about x, y and z through a shared centre. The picks are
    points, circles or arcs (their centres), and they must coincide.
- `SetJointValue(hj, v, remember, dof)`, `SetJointLimits(hj, limited, lo, hi, dof)`,
  `SweepClearance(hj, samples, dof)`.
- Properties: one row per motion: value (`joint-value`, `joint-value1`, `joint-value2`),
  scrub, limits, sweep.
- `G`: X, Y and Z pick the motion. Without one, a cylindrical joint turns, a planar joint
  slides in its plane, and a ball turns like a trackball.
- Tests: `test/group/joints` (each type's pose, limits per motion, save and reload).

### Task 2: gear and rack couplings

- `Joint::Type::GEAR` couples motion 0 of two joints: `driven = ratio × driver`.
- New Group ▸ Gear Coupling (`joint-gear`) takes two entities on parts that joints move.
  - The default ratio comes from the picks: two pitch circles give `-rA/rB`; a circle on a
    revolute with a slider gives `r·π/180` mm per degree; otherwise 1.
  - Properties edits the ratio.
- Setting either joint sets the other. Limits clamp the pair together, and a clearance sweep
  moves both.
- Tests: two gears turn opposite at the ratio; a rack slides with its pinion; limits; prune
  when a joint goes.

### Task 3: the fit table

- **Storage:** document-wide named clearances (`SK.fit`, `AddFit` records: `Fit.name`,
  `Fit.clearance`), saved once a file uses or edits them.
- **Defaults:** press −0.10, slide 0.20, loose 0.40 mm.
- **Editing:** Inspect ▸ Fit Table opens it in Properties, where each fit's clearance can be
  set, and fits added, renamed and deleted.
- **Tests:** defaults, editing, save and reload, undo.

### Task 4: fit joints

- New Group ▸ Fit Joint (`group-fit`) works from the active sketch, a closed profile in a
  workplane (the shared profile). It needs one picked item on each of two parts, the male
  first.
  - **Pair of groups:** it adds a `FIT` group to each part. The male's group unions the
    profile extruded by the depth; the female's cuts it offset by the fit's clearance
    (`BRepOffsetAPI_MakeOffset`, arc joins) and deeper by the clearance.
  - **Direction:** towards the female part.
- **Styles:**
  - **Peg:** the male gets the profile, the female a pocket. This covers pin-hole,
    dovetail, slide and snap, with the profile drawn to suit.
  - **Dowel:** both parts get a pocket, half the depth each side, for a separate pin.
- **Properties of either group:** fit (from the table), depth, style, flip. Each edit
  changes both groups.
- **Kernel:** OCCT only. Other kernels report it in Problems.
- **Tests:** the male volume grows by the profile's area × depth; the female pocket's width
  is the profile's plus twice the clearance. Also editing the fit, and save/reload.

### Task 5: split with automatic joints

- New Group ▸ Split Part (`group-split`) takes a workplane or a planar face and a part
  (the active group's).
  - **Two groups:** the part keeps the side the plane's normal points away from, and a new
    part takes the other side (two `SPLIT` groups).
  - **Dowels:** optional automatic joints, two dowels on the cut face's long axis, or one at
    its centre, kept clear of its edges, with Ø and the fit editable. A cut face too small
    for a dowel is noted in Problems.
- **Tests:**
  - the halves' volumes add up to the whole;
  - the dowel pockets exist on both halves;
  - a narrow cut face gets one dowel, or none and a note.
