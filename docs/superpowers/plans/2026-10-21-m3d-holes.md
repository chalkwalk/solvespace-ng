# M3d Holes and Threads Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans (inline; the owner
> asked to continue with the rest of M3, 2026-10-21).

**Goal:** a hole group (AGENTS.md section 6: "Hole (plain, counterbore, countersink, modelled
ISO threads) — derived: axis, centre, top/bottom points, counterbore plane").

**Architecture** (the patterns of M3b/M3c):
- **Group.** `Group::Type::HOLE` = 5404. It holds `std::vector<hEntity> points` (saved
  `Group.points`), the hole centres: points in a workplane, typically a sketch on a face. It
  also holds `Group::HoleSpec hole` (saved `Group.hole.*`):
  - kind: simple, counterbore or countersink;
  - diameter, and depth (0 = through all);
  - head diameter, head depth (counterbore) and head angle (countersink, default 90°);
  - thread: an ISO metric coarse nominal size, 0 for none, and its pitch;
  - flip.
- **Direction.** A hole goes along −normal of its point's workplane (into the face it was
  sketched on), or +normal when flipped. That is deterministic, with no B-rep query; flip is
  for the other case.
- **Kernel** (`Op::HOLE`; `KernelJob::holes` with point, direction and seat name; and the
  spec):
  - Each hole is a tool fused from a bore cylinder (through: as long as the previous solid's
    bounding box along the direction) and a counterbore cylinder or countersink cone. Each
    tool starts a little above the point, so it never meets the face coplanar.
  - With a thread, the bore is the minor diameter `D − 1.0825·P`, and an ISO 60° groove is
    swept along a helix of pitch `P` (as the helix group's pipe sweep, axis binormal),
    reaching the major diameter. The groove is 0.75·P wide at the minor radius and P/8 at
    the major.
  - All tools are cut from the previous solid.
  - The seat face (counterbore floor, or the plane where the countersink meets the bore) is
    named with the derived seat entity: a planar unnamed face of the result in the seat's
    plane.
  - Failure, or a hole that cuts nothing, is a problem. With SolveSpace's kernel the solid
    passes through.
- **Derived entities** for each point, as `Remap(point, …)`:
  - `REMAP_HOLE_TOP` (the centre on the face);
  - `REMAP_HOLE_BOTTOM` (the centre at the depth: through holes use the previous solid's
    extent along the direction, from its mesh);
  - `REMAP_HOLE_AXIS` (line top → bottom);
  - `REMAP_HOLE_SEAT` (a face at the seat depth, normal against the direction; at the top
    for a simple hole).

  They are numeric copies, current every regeneration, and always exist.
- **UI.**
  - New Group ▸ Hole (`group-hole`, `Shift+H` in Modern: Onshape has no binding; `H` is
    horizontal);
  - Properties: kind radios, diameter, depth (blank or 0 = through), head fields per kind,
    thread (`M3`, or `none`), flip;
  - Outliner icon `hole.svg`; shelf.

## Global constraints
As M3c's (green on every commit; old files unchanged; OCCT-only code guarded; derived
entities always exist; values-only jobs).

## Review focus
1. A through hole on a solid made of two bodies (union): it goes through both.
2. A point not on the solid: the hole cuts nothing, with a problem; nothing crashes.
3. A thread bigger than the wall: the cut still works, or fails with a problem.
4. Changing kind from counterbore to simple: the seat entity stays (constraints kept).
5. Save/reload keeps every hole field.

## Tasks
1. **Group, storage, command, derived entities** (`test/group/hole/test.cpp`):
   - made from points in a workplane;
   - refused for nothing selected, a free 3D point, or SolveSpace's kernel;
   - spec save/reload;
   - axis/top/bottom/seat entities, which follow a moved point.
2. **Kernel**:
   - through, blind, counterbore, countersink and two holes: exact volumes;
   - the seat face named;
   - a hole off the solid is a problem;
   - an M4 thread: volume between the minor and major bores;
   - worker regeneration matches.
3. **UI**: menu, key, shelf, icon, Properties fields; `test/ngui/hole/test.cpp`; AGENTS.md.
